# Jie - Prepare the toppings

## Ingredients

- Tomato sauce
- Mozzarella cheese
- Mushrooms
- Ham

## Steps

1. Slice the mushrooms.
2. Cut the ham into small pieces.
3. Grate the mozzarella cheese.
4. Prepare the tomato sauce.

## Notes

- Do not add too many toppings.
- Keep the toppings evenly distributed.
