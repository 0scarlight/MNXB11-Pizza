# here's a recipe for making the dough

Ingredients:
- pizza flour (600 g)  
- instant yeast (2 tsp)  
- salt (2 tsp)  
- sugar (20g )  
- extra virgin olive oil (4 tbsp)  
- warm water (330 ml)  

Instructions:  
1. Mix the dry ingredients (flour, yeast, sugar and salt) in a large bowl.  
2. Add oil and water and mix.  
3. Sprinkle flour on a flat surface and form the dough into a ball. Knead until smooth and shape into a ball again.  
4. Cover a bowl and dough with a layer of olive oil. Let it rise in warm place for 1-2 hours.  
5. Divide the dough into smaller parts and on floured surface flatten the ball, shaping it into a circle, and spread it further using a rolling pin.
