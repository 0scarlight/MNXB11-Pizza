# Quick pizza recipe  

The humble pizza is a dish of wide acclaim and a storied history. In the current era it is widely accepted to consist of a round and flat bread base layered with a spiced and velvety tomato based sauce covered with cheese and toppings to one's likings, but the current pizza is a far cry form what the dish originally was and what is lost much of what its symbolism. To make a great pizza, one must first leran and understand the history of and the figures responsible for this mighty food. 

## Chapter 1: The pyromancer _Arthas_

Arguably the most important part of the pizza, the bread, was first created by the archwizard _Arthas Menethil_ in a desperate attempt to feed the people of Atmora and atone for his sins. For you see, _Arthas_ wasn't a normal archwizard, he was the best pyromancer of his time. Unfortunately, due to an unforeseen **event**, _Arthas_ had destroyed most of the continent's food productionwhich would surely doom the remaining population. 

In order to properly atone for his wrongdoings and save the people of Atmora, _Arthas_ set out on a long voyage to the lands of ~~Neverwinter~~ in search of the wise _Patrick Star_ to get advice from their great hoard of knowledge. After a long and treacherous journey, Arthus arrives at hte mountain in which _Patrick_  resides to confer with the wise starfish. _Patrick_, e'er so wise, gave _Arthus_ the solution to his problem and instructed him to make use of the grass-like weeds that had sprung up all over Atmora as a consequence of the **event**. _Arthas_ would then have to mill the plump seeds of the grass into a fine dust and combine it with water to make a kind loose blob which he'd have to expose to the blazing hot flames that he alone could produce. 

_Arthas_, enlightened by the great _Patrick_, returned to Atmora and spread of this new thing and managed to save most of the population of Atmora. _Arthus_ shared this discovery and dubbed the food "**Bread**" which then spread across the realm along with the story of the redeemed _Arthas_ and the great _Patrick_.

## Chapter 2: The Whale Riders and the first **good** cheese

Before the discovery of the tomato sauce by the Golems of Australia, another critical ingredients of the pizza was added to the bread that _Arthas_ had discovered was added. Namely the cheese. Despite many a popular accounts, the whale riders weren't actually the creators of cheese but rather they popularised it. 

For those unaware, the whale riders of yesteryear were a much different group compared to the secluded fanatics of, what they call, the post-R'lyeh:an whale riders.The pre-R'lyeh:an riders where a cube spanning trade empire and was the forefront of advancement. They made good use of their loyal companions, the whales, to travel the cube and to both expand their empire and establish new trade routes. In order to properly and safely embark on these voyages they made good use of _Arthas'_ bread and cheese made from goats milk, however little did they know that the goats cheese would whisper to them and, inevitably lead them astray which would waylay a group of traders. After months on the sea these traders were at wit's end and had quickly started to run low on their food supplies when, suddenly, they found themselves atop a weird looking island in the middle of nowhere. 

Desperate for any respite, the traders explored this island and found many ruins and a giant temple. The imposing temple had some weird inscriptions on them and, despite their efforts, they couldn't get inside. That's when one of the traders took a closer look at the inscriptions and noticed some familiar patterns depicting a large octopus like creature, and some whales with arrows pointing towards some strange looking cheese. Seemingly inspired by theses inscriptions, the trader proceeded to harvest the milk from their whales and turned it into whale cheese.

In a almost trance like state, the trader then added this cheese atop a piece of bread from their dwindling bread stores and presented it to the remaining group of traders. Little did they know that the island they found themselves on was the island of R'lyeh and by consuming this cheesy bread they had unleashed a great evil upon the world and effectively doomed their civilisation. Curiously, though they managed to survive this encounter and managed to spread the word of the **good** whale cheese to most of the world and managed to mitigate the damaged that they caused. Eventually their teachings of the **good** cheese had managed to dethrone goats cheese as the rightful heir to the name "cheese" and as such it is rarely referred to by any other name than just "cheese" in the current era. As to what happened to these traders, or as they are more commonly known as the disciples of Cheese, after they had spread the word of the **good** cheese, no one really knows, though there have been rumours that they still live on to this day and protect their sacred whales until they are needed once again.  

## Chapter 3: The Golems of Australia  

Now onto the last major pizza ingredient, the tomato sauce. As alluded to earlier, the tomato sauce has it's origins in the green island known as Australia. The origin of the tomato plant is still a mystery, as is much else about the first people of Australia, but what we do know is that at some point in the last 10 000 years tomato sauce was invented and a multitude of inorganic life, in the form of Golems, had proliferated across Australia. 

When Australia was first discovered by the rest of the world, the people of Australia were already long gone and all that was left of them were the Golems and the copious amount of ruins they left behind. This might come as a shock to many of you, both the Golems and the ruins contain heaps of archeologically important information, however, it seems a large amount of the information have a lot of contradictions and idiosyncrasies which point to the information being altered or corrupted in some way, shape or, form.

This has lead many scholars to settle on Australia to try and piece together the true story of the island and as such the the introduction of the cheesy bread to the Golems, who combined it with the tomato sauce to form the first pizza. Curiously, when the other Golems were first shown a pizza many of them showed a sign of familiarity with the dish, even mentioning it by name and describing eerily similar, yet different, account of the creation of the pizza. This has baffled many a scholar and still remains a mystery to this day.    

## Chapter 4: Modern Day

Now that you have been thoroughly educated on the history of the pizza it's time to describe how to actually make a pizza. Here I'm going to give you two recipes for two different variations on the pizza, a more simple and common open style pizza and a more traditional enclosed pizza:

### Simple Open Style Pizza (15 min):

#### Ingredients:  
* Dough (preferably homemade)
* 1.5 Ladles or 2.536 fl oz of tomato Sauce  
* 5.5 Ounces or 779.612 ct of your preferred cheese
* (optional) Any toppings 

#### Recipe:
1. Shape the dough into a a circular shape
2. Evenly spread the tomato sauce on the dough using a iron roller
3. Add the cheese and any other toppings to the pizza 
4. Bake the pizza in an oven heated to 200° C for 10 minutes
5. Remove from oven and cut into slices using a poleaxe
6. Enjoy!!!

### Traditional Enclosed Pizza (45 min):

#### Ingredients: 
* 1 Loaf of Unleavened Bread made from winter wheat 
* 1 Bottle of _Heritage^TM^ tomato sauce_ 
* 1 Quarter of a wheel of eastern whale cheese 

#### Recipe:
1. Make a cavity in the Bread by poking a hole in the side and blowing high speed air (like that of a slipstream spell) through said hole 
2. Inject the _Heritage^TM^ tomato sauce_ into the cavity using the bottle's cap
3. Sprinkle crumbled cheese across the top of the bread making sure to completely cover the top. A tip to make the cheese stay in place is casting a barrier spell around the bread. 
4. Either get a fire dragon to flame the stuffed bread until the cheese is melted, Alternatively you can accomplish a similar thing by using a high intensity fire spell (like volcanic hands) to achieve a similar result.
5. Enjoy!!!
