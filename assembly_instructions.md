# Quick pizza recipe  

The humble pizza is a dish of wide acclaim and a storied history. In the current era it is widely accepted to consist of a round and flat bread base layered with a spiced and velvety tomato based sauce covered with cheese and toppings to one's likings, but the current pizza is a far cry form what the dish originally was and what is lost much of what its symbolism. To make a great pizza, one must first leran and understand the history of and the figures responsible for this mighty food. 

## Chapter 1: The pyromancer _Arthas_

Arguably the most important part of the pizza, the bread, was first created by the archwizard _Arthas Menethil_ in a desperate attempt to feed the people of Atmora and atone for his sins. For you see, _Arthas_ wasn't a normal archwizard, he was the best pyromancer of his time. Unfortunately, due to an unforeseen **event**, _Arthas_ had destroyed most of the continent's food productionwhich would surely doom the remaining population. 

In order to properly atone for his wrongdoings and save the people of Atmora, _Arthas_ set out on a long voyage to the lands of ~~Neverwinter~~ in search of the wise _Patrick Star_ to get advice from their great hoard of knowledge. After a long and treacherous journey, Arthus arrives at hte mountain in which _Patrick_  resides to confer with the wise starfish. _Patrick_, e'er so wise, gave _Arthus_ the solution to his problem and instructed him to make use of the grass-like weeds that had sprung up all over Atmora as a consequence of the **event**. _Arthas_ would then have to mill the plump seeds of the grass into a fine dust and combine it with water to make a kind loose blob which he'd have to expose to the blazing hot flames that he alone could produce. 

_Arthas_, enlightened by the great _Patrick_, returned to Atmora and spread of this new thing and managed to save most of the population of Atmora. _Arthus_ shared this discovery and dubbed the food "**Bread**" which then spread across the realm along with the story of the redeemed _Arthas_ and the great _Patrick_.
