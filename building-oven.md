# Building the Pizza Oven 
## A step by step guide

### Materials reuqired
* Red bricks
* clay stock bricks
* face bricks (full length and  half length)
* concrete slabs
* shutter board
* ready mix concrete 
* ready builders mix
* precast concrete lintels
* perlite mix
* hardboard 
* mortar
* plaster mix
* dry plaster sand
* floor tiles
* perlite plaster
* kindling wood

### Step 1: Preparing the Base
Find a flat area for the base which should be 1.5 x 1.5m. Lay down the foundation of **conrete slabs**, which should be above ground level. 

Then construct a box out of **shutter board** with dimensions of 1.42m x 1.37m and position it on the base area.

### Step 2: Casting the Slab
Mix bags of **ready mix concrete** and cast it directly onto the levelled surface inside the wooden box. 

Leave the concrete for at least 24 hours to cure before continuing. The shutter box can be removed at 3 days.


### Step 3: Building the Walls
Build the walls using **red bricks** for the outer layer and **clay stock bricks** for the inner layer. Use 5x 40kg bags of **ready builders mix** as the brick 'glue'. 

Make the two side walls 9 bricks high for the inner layer and 10 bricks high for the outer layer. Lay 13x 100x 75x 1200 mm **precast concrete lintels** across the inner layer. 


### Step 4: Building the Oven
Build the outer layer up by 3 courses. Each course consists of four and a half bricks. 

Use **perlite mix** to screed over the lintels. This will provide insulation and smooth the base of the oven floor.

### Step 5: Continuing the Oven Walls
Use **face bricks** to build up the inner layer. This type of bricks can easily withstand the heat of the oven. 

Lay the bricks vertically (known as a soldier course). Make the joints as small as possible to reduce the amount of cement exposed to the heat.

Lay out the barrel vault bricks on a flat surface. This semi-circle shape will be the back of the arched ceiling. The height should not exceed 500 mm. Cut the bricks as necessary to build the arch.

### Step 6: Building the Barrel Vault
Cut two matching peices of **hardboard** for making the framwork of building the arches. Screw same length peices of wood between the boards, around 150-200 mm apart. 

Lay the bricks on the first arch resting on top of the framemwork. Once happy, use **mortar** to 'glue' them together. 

The brickwork is self supporting hence the framework can be removed immediately.

### Step 7: Completing the Vault
Move the wooden framework along and build the next arch directly next to the first. Repeat this four times. Then start to build the outer layer of the vault using **clay stock bricks**. 

### Step 8: Building the Doorway
The doorway and its walls should be smaller than the oven so that heat does not escape. Build the doorway walls using soldier course. The width should be about 500 mm and the height should be about 270 mm.

### Step 9: Finishing the Oven Floor
Screed the doorway floor with **plaster mix**. Make this layer around 10 mm thick.

Lay **dry plaster sand** inside the oven and smooth it to be level with the doorway screed.

Lay the oven **floor tiles** on top of the plaster sand bed. These tiles should remain loose to allow for thermal expansion. The tiles in the doorway can be installed using **tile adhesive**.

### Step 10: Continuing with Doorway and Chimney
Using the same method before, build an arch over the doorway. Use **half face bricks** for this.

Build the chimney behind the arch. This should have a cross sectional area of one brick. The taller the chimney the better.

### Step 11: Finishing Touches
Plaster the outside of the oven using **perlite plaster** to insulate it. Go over the perlite plaster with regular plaster to give a smoother finish. 

A wooden door to the oven can be added. This door can be loose and will plug in the doorway.

### Step 12: Firing Up the Oven
Build a small fire using **kindling wood** in the centre of the oven. Wait for it to reach a temperature of 260°C to 290°C.
